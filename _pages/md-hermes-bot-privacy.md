# Privacy Policy for MD Hermes Bot

Effective date: August 24, 2026

MD Hermes Bot is the public app identity for a private personal automation
operated by the Google account owner as part of the Hermes workflow. It is not
a public SaaS product and is not offered for third-party use.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact: mengwei.assistant@gmail.com

## Gmail and Google account data access

MD Hermes Bot requests access to the account owner's Gmail data only as needed
to:

- read unread or forwarded emails;
- mark processed messages as read; and
- send summaries or responses requested by the account owner.

The app does not access Gmail or other Google account data for advertising,
profiling, resale, or any purpose unrelated to the account owner's personal
automation.

## Data processing and storage

Email data accessed through Google APIs is processed locally by Hermes for the
account owner's personal automation workflow. MD Hermes Bot does not sell,
share, transfer, or disclose Gmail data, other Google user data, or data
derived from them to any third party.

## User control and revocation

The account owner can revoke MD Hermes Bot's access to their Google account at
any time from Google's permissions page:
[https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
