# Privacy Policy for Kai Email Forward Router

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Kai Email Forward Router is private personal automation for the Google account owner. It is not a public SaaS product and is not offered for third-party use.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact: mengwei.assistant@gmail.com

## Gmail access and use

Kai Email Forward Router accesses Gmail only as needed to:

- read unread or forwarded emails;
- mark processed messages as read; and
- send summaries or responses requested by the account owner.

The app does not access Gmail for advertising, profiling, resale, or unrelated purposes.

## Data processing and storage

Email data is processed locally by Hermes for the personal automation workflow. The app does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose Gmail data or derived data to third parties.

## User control and revocation

The account owner can revoke the app's Google access at any time from Google's permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
