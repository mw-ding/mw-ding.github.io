# Terms of Service for MD Hermes Bot

Effective date: August 24, 2026

operated by the Google account owner as part of the Hermes workflow. It is not
a public SaaS product and is not offered for third-party use.

## Purpose

MD Hermes Bot helps the account owner process Gmail messages by reading unread
or forwarded emails, marking processed messages as read, and sending summaries
or responses requested by the account owner.

MD Hermes Bot is intended only for the account owner's personal automation. No
third party is authorized to use or rely on the app. MD Hermes Bot does not
sell, share, transfer, or disclose Gmail data, other Google user data, or data
derived from them to any third party.

MD Hermes Bot is provided as-is, without warranties, service commitments,
uptime guarantees, or support obligations.

## Revoking access

any time from Google's permissions page:
