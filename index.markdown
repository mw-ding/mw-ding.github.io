## MD Hermes Bot

**MD Hermes Bot** is a private personal automation used by the Google account
owner as part of the Hermes workflow. It is not a public SaaS product and is not
offered for third-party use. It accesses Gmail only to read unread or forwarded
messages, mark processed messages as read, and send summaries or responses that
the account owner has requested. Email data is processed locally by Hermes; the
app does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose Gmail data or derived data to
any third party.
